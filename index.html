How Trialwise works

The short version

Add a free trial's start date and length. Trialwise calculates the last safe day to cancel, shows it in plain language, and keeps your cancel link nearby. No account. No cloud. No tracking.

How cancel-by dates work

Start Oct 1
→
Cancel by Oct 7
→
Billing starts Oct 8

A 7-day trial that starts on October 1 includes October 1 through October 7. Trialwise treats October 7 as the last safe cancellation day and October 8 as the day billing may begin.

Providers have their own cut-off times, so earlier is safer. This tool is a reminder aid, not a guarantee of a provider's billing behavior.

Reminders you can see

Your board shows deadlines without any notification permission. Where your browser supports it, you can also opt in to system notifications. They can only run while the app is open or running in the background; an offline PWA cannot wake itself when fully closed. The calendar export is the best fallback for reminders outside the app.

Your data & backups

Trials stay in this browser's local storage. JSON backups preserve every field for moving or restoring. CSV exports are spreadsheet-friendly and can be imported back. Cancel-page links open the provider's site only when you click them.
